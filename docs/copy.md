  site (usually a Fraunces serif italic in clay or brass — the soft accent

  something is a click target.

## About page

### Page hero

- **Title:** About Moonbloom

### How It All Began

- **Heading:** How It All Began

- **Paragraph 1:** Moonbloom started in 2023, when we set down roots on a stretch of Sonoma Valley land and got to work. What began as a backyard project — a few rows of vegetables, a small flock of chickens — has slowly grown into the roadside stand you'll find on Arnold Drive today.

- **Paragraph 2:** We're still figuring it out, season by season. Some weeks we have more eggs than we know what to do with; some weeks the garden runs wild. We bring what we have to the stand and try to leave it a little better than we found it.

### Pull-quote (oversized italic line, brass color, lots of whitespace)

*The land teaches us.*

### Our Growing Philosophy

- **Heading:** Our Growing Philosophy

- **Paragraph 1:** We grow without synthetic pesticides or herbicides — just compost, cover crops, and a willingness to let the seasons set the pace. The chickens get the run of the place. The garden gets time.

- **Paragraph 2:** What ends up at the stand is whatever the land decided to give us that week. We think it tastes better that way.

### Values section

- *Italic eyebrow (centered):* A few things we believe

- **01 — Sustainably Grown**
  - No synthetic chemicals, no shortcuts. We feed the soil with compost and cover crops and let it do its work, season by season.

- **02 — Locally Rooted**
  - Everything we grow stays in the valley. The stand sits at the edge of our driveway in Glen Ellen — close enough that the eggs are usually still warm.

- **03 — Seasonally Inspired**
  - We follow the calendar that nature writes. Spring greens, summer tomatoes, fall squash — what is at the stand is whatever the season is offering, and we wouldn't have it any other way.

### Meet the Farmers

- **Heading:** Meet the Farmers
- *Italic name (clay):* Brittney Harvey & John Singleton

- **Bio paragraph 1:** Brittney and John traded a busier life for a quieter one in 2023, swapping the city for a few acres in Glen Ellen and a steady rotation of small projects with feathers, leaves, and dirt.

- **Bio paragraph 2:** Moonbloom is a side-of-the-road, side-of-life kind of farm — not their day job, but where real care goes. Stop by the stand on a Friday morning and one of them will probably be there to say hello.

## Things to weigh in on

A few lines that may benefit from a second pass — feel free to edit, replace, or keep as-is:

1. **Homepage hero subtitle** — *"Grown with love in the Valley of the Moon."* This is one of the few lines still in the soft-poetic register; everything else in the rewrite is more direct and grounded. Consider whether to keep the moon reference (matches the brand name) or swap for something tighter.

2. **Homepage hero buttons** — "See the Harvest" goes to the What We Sell page. "Harvest" implies flowers/produce, which doesn't quite cover eggs and baked goods. Could be "See What We Sell" or "See What's at the Stand" — but "See the Harvest" has more voice.

3. **About pull-quote** — *"The land teaches us."* This was pulled from body copy and elevated as a display moment between the two story blocks. If a different line would be better as the centerpiece, easy to swap.

4. **About — Brittney & John bio** — Generic-but-honest two-paragraph bio written without specific details. You'll likely want to replace with something specific to who you are and why you're doing this.

5. **About — three values** — "Sustainably Grown / Locally Rooted / Seasonally Inspired" is okay but the framing was inherited. If there's something more honest to say, or if values feel like overreach for a small side-of-life farm, easy to cut or rewrite.

6. **Gallery captions and "Flowers" filter** — All 16 captions reference flowers. Need rewriting (or letting real photos drive the captions) when photos arrive. The "Flowers" filter category should probably be replaced.

7. **Footer tagline** — *"Rooted in seasonality, grown for real life."* Slightly aphoristic; this one pre-existed and was not part of the recent rewrite. Worth a second look.

8. **What We Sell — Baked Goods body** — *"Sourdough loaves, scones, the occasional pie"* implies specific things you may or may not actually bake. Easily swappable.

9. **AboutPreview link "Learn More"** — Standard CTA. Could be more in voice ("Read the longer story", "More on us") — your call.

10. **Hero facts** — "SINCE 2023 · SUSTAINABLY GROWN · SONOMA VALLEY" — these are short marketing-style facts. If you'd rather replace with something more specific (e.g. "EGGS · PRODUCE · BAKED GOODS"), easy to swap.
